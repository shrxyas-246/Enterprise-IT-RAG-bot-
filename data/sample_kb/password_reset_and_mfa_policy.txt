PASSWORD RESET AND MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA) POLICY

Owner: IT Security
Applies to: All employees, contractors and interns
Last reviewed: 2026-08-20
Document ID: IT-KB-SEC-002


1. PURPOSE

This policy explains the password requirements for company accounts, how to reset a forgotten or expired password, how account lockout works, and how to set up and recover multi-factor authentication (MFA).


2. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS

All company account passwords must meet these rules:

- Minimum length of 14 characters.
- Must include at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number and one special character.
- Must not contain your name, username or the word "company".
- Must not match any of your last 10 passwords.
- Passwords expire every 180 days. You will receive reminder emails 14 days, 7 days and 1 day before expiry.

Passphrases are encouraged. Example format: four or more random words joined together with a number and symbol.

Never write your password down, store it in a plain text file, or share it with anyone, including IT staff. IT will never ask for your password.

The approved password manager for work accounts is the company-licensed password vault available in Company Software Center.


3. SELF-SERVICE PASSWORD RESET

If you forgot your password or it has expired, use self-service reset:

Step 1: Go to the password reset portal at passwordreset.company.internal.
Step 2: Enter your company email address.
Step 3: Verify your identity using your registered MFA method (authenticator app push or code).
Step 4: Enter a new password that meets the requirements in Section 2.
Step 5: Wait up to 15 minutes for the new password to sync to all systems, including VPN, email and Wi-Fi.
Step 6: Update the saved password on your phone email app and any other devices to avoid lockout.

If you cannot complete self-service reset (for example, you lost your MFA device), contact the IT Service Desk. The Service Desk will verify your identity through a video call with your photo ID and your manager's confirmation before resetting your password.


4. ACCOUNT LOCKOUT

- Your account is locked after 5 failed sign-in attempts within 15 minutes.
- A locked account unlocks automatically after 30 minutes.
- To unlock immediately, use the self-service reset portal or call the IT Service Desk at extension 4357.
- Repeated lockouts are often caused by an old password saved on a phone, tablet or mapped network drive. Update or remove old saved passwords on all devices.
- More than 3 lockouts in 24 hours triggers an automatic security review by IT Security.


5. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)

MFA is mandatory for all accounts. It is required for email, VPN, the HR portal, finance applications and all cloud applications.

Approved MFA methods, in order of preference:

1. Authenticator app push notification (recommended).
2. Authenticator app 6-digit code.
3. Hardware security key (available on request for travelling staff and administrators).

SMS text message codes are NOT allowed as an MFA method because they can be intercepted.


6. SETTING UP MFA FOR THE FIRST TIME

Step 1: Install the approved authenticator app on your phone from the official app store.
Step 2: On your laptop, go to mysecurity.company.internal and sign in.
Step 3: Click "Add sign-in method" and choose "Authenticator app".
Step 4: Scan the QR code shown on screen with the authenticator app.
Step 5: Approve the test notification to finish setup.
Step 6: Download and safely store your 10 one-time backup codes.

New joiners must complete MFA setup within 3 days of their start date, or their account will be restricted.


7. NEW PHONE OR LOST PHONE

Getting a new phone (old phone still available):
- Before wiping your old phone, sign in to mysecurity.company.internal, add the authenticator app on the new phone, and then remove the old phone.

Lost or stolen phone:
- Sign in using one of your backup codes and remove the lost device immediately.
- If you do not have backup codes, call the IT Service Desk at extension 4357. IT will verify your identity and issue a temporary access pass valid for 8 hours so you can register a new device.
- Report a lost phone that contains company email to IT Security within 1 hour at security@company.internal.


8. MFA TROUBLESHOOTING

Not receiving push notifications:
- Check that notifications are enabled for the authenticator app.
- Make sure your phone has an internet connection.
- Use the 6-digit code from the app instead of the push notification.

6-digit code rejected:
- Make sure your phone's date and time are set automatically. Codes fail if the phone clock is wrong.

Unexpected MFA prompts you did not request:
- Do NOT approve the prompt. Unexpected prompts may mean someone else knows your password.
- Change your password immediately using the reset portal and report the incident to security@company.internal.


9. SUPPORT CONTACTS

IT Service Desk: extension 4357 (HELP), available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
IT portal ticket category: Accounts > Password or MFA Issue.
IT Security: security@company.internal
